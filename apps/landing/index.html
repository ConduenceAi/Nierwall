PRIVACY, IN PLAIN WORDS

Know what goes where.

Your monitoring and plan checks run locally. Your keys stay in your wallet. Connected services receive only what their features need:

  • Private AI. Messages and relevant positions are encrypted to verified NEAR AI TEEs, using wallet nicknames instead of addresses.
  • On-chain reads. RPC providers see the addresses they query. Prices come from public data services.
  • Memory stays private. Facts are pulled out by the same verified NEAR AI TEE, and memory search runs on your Mac.
  • Optional connections. Telegram receives alerts at your chosen detail level.
  • Cross-chain moves. A transfer can link wallets on-chain. Nierwall warns you before you sign.
